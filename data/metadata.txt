Dataset: Health Logs

STRUCTURE:
-----------
Total Records: 1500
Total Users: 50
Days per User: 30

COLUMNS:
-----------
1. user_id (string): Unique user identifier (format: user_XXX)
2. user_name (string): User's full name
3. age (integer): User's age
4. date (string): Date in YYYY-MM-DD format
5. steps (integer): Daily step count
6. sleep_hours (float): Hours of sleep (rounded to 1 decimal)
7. heart_rate (integer): Average heart rate in beats per minute (bpm)

STATISTICS:
-----------
Steps:
  Mean: ~8000
  Std: ~2000
  Min: 0
  Max: ~16000

Sleep Hours:
  Mean: 7.5
  Std: 1.0
  Min: 4.0
  Max: 12.0

Heart Rate:
  Mean: 72
  Std: 8
  Min: 50
  Max: 100

Age Distribution:
  Range: 18-75 years
  Groups: 18-30, 31-50, 51-75

ANOMALIES:
-----------
5 users have intentional anomalies for testing anomaly detection.
These anomalies occur in the last 3 days of the dataset (days 28-30).
Drop percentage: 50-80% for the affected metric.

Anomaly users:
- user_005 (Jessica Martinez): steps anomaly
- user_012 (Matthew Jackson): sleep_hours anomaly
- user_015 (Stephanie Martin): steps anomaly
- user_023 (Samantha Hall): heart_rate anomaly (increase)
- user_028 (Amber Lopez): sleep_hours anomaly

Detection Rate: 10% (5 out of 50 users)

NOTES:
-----------
- All users have realistic names (50 unique names)
- Age-adjusted baselines for health metrics
- Weekend effect: 30% reduction in steps on weekends
- Anomalies are diverse across different metrics (steps, sleep, heart rate)
- Heart rate anomalies are INCREASES (not decreases) indicating stress
